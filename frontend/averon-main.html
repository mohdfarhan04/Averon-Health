Medical Credentialing

ABOUT AVERON

Averon Healthcare Solutions partners with providers and groups to streamline Credentialing, Contracting, and EDI Operations - combining every step of provider enrollment into one seamless process, so your practice can start delivering care and billing from day one.

About Us

Averon Healthcare Solutions was founded on a straightforward insight: providers lose valuable time and revenue when enrollment and credentialing are handled reactively. We built Averon to change that — bringing structure, speed, and transparency to a process that too often stalls practice growth.

We are a specialized credentialing and provider enrollment consultancy serving physician practices, group practices, and healthcare organizations across the United States. From initial CAQH setup and Medicare/Medicaid enrollment to commercial payer contracting and ongoing re-credentialing, we manage the full lifecycle so your providers can focus entirely on patient care.

  • Credentialing & Enrollment Expertise: End-to-end provider enrollment with Medicare, Medicaid, and All commercial payers.
  • Compliance-First Approach: HIPAA-compliant workflows, secure document handling, and meticulous primary source verification at every step.
  • Proactive Expirables Management: Tracking licenses, DEA certificates, board certifications, and malpractice renewals — so nothing lapses on your watch.
  • Faster Time-to-Revenue: Streamlined submissions, payer follow-ups, and issue resolution that get your providers billing sooner.
